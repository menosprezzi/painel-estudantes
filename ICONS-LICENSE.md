# Ícones Lucide

Os SVGs inline em `src/_includes/icons.njk` vêm da [biblioteca Lucide](https://lucide.dev/icons/). Eles são incorporados ao HTML gerado, sem JavaScript, CDN ou dependência de execução.

Fonte dos desenhos: [users](https://lucide.dev/icons/users), [terminal](https://lucide.dev/icons/terminal), [code](https://lucide.dev/icons/code), [calendar](https://lucide.dev/icons/calendar), [music](https://lucide.dev/icons/music), [download](https://lucide.dev/icons/download), [git-branch](https://lucide.dev/icons/git-branch), [plus](https://lucide.dev/icons/plus), [check](https://lucide.dev/icons/check) e [upload](https://lucide.dev/icons/upload).
